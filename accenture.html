Accenture

As an intern in Accenture’s Strategy & Consulting Operations team, I contribute to projects exploring how companies can improve operations and turn technological possibilities into practical business value.

What I'm working on

Working as part of a consulting team has given me exposure to complex business processes and the practical questions behind operations transformation.

My work touches on process improvement, supply-chain optimization opportunities and AI use-case evaluation, alongside quantitative business analysis and business-case development.

Where strategy meets execution

Understand the problem

Break down operational processes, identify bottlenecks and clarify the business need before focusing on technology.

Find opportunities

Examine where digital and AI-enabled approaches might improve decisions, workflows and operational performance.

Evaluate business value

Support structured analysis and business cases to weigh potential impact and implementation considerations.

Work across disciplines

Collaborate with consulting, AI and technology specialists to connect technical ideas with business priorities.

What I'm learning

The most interesting lesson is that a promising technology is not the same as a useful business solution. The challenge is understanding how the organization really works and what would make change possible.

I'm also learning how important clear, structured communication is when working with multidisciplinary teams.
