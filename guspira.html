Guspira Lleure

Together with a group of friends, I co-founded a non-profit bringing educational summer programmes to children across the Moianès. What started as an idea has grown into something that families, counsellors and municipalities can rely on.

How it started

We wanted to create quality opportunities for children to learn, play and connect through educational leisure — while also making room for local young people to take on meaningful responsibilities as counsellors.

Turning the idea into a functioning organization meant building a team, working with municipalities and putting the practical foundations in place.

What we've built

160+children in summer 2026
3summer camps
20+counsellors
€40K+annual budget

In 2026 we ran programmes in Castellterçol, Castellcir and l'Estany. We also secured over €4,000 in public funding and maintained partnerships with three municipalities.

What I do

Planning & finance

Setting priorities, helping manage the annual budget, allocating resources and keeping operations financially sustainable.

People & leadership

Recruiting, training and coordinating a team of more than 20 counsellors with different responsibilities.

Partnerships

Building relationships with municipalities, public institutions, families and other collaborators.

Operations

Coordinating multiple programmes, activity calendars, staffing, communication and practical logistics.

Building a digital presence

Guspira also needs clear online communication so families and collaborators can find reliable information about its programmes. I've been involved in the organization's digital and coordination initiatives alongside the team.

What I've learned

Building Guspira has taught me that having an idea is only the beginning. Making something work means listening carefully, managing trade-offs, adapting when plans change and making sure people can count on what you've promised.

It has also reminded me how rewarding it is to build something alongside people who care about the same goal.
