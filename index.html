About me

Hi! I'm Oriol, thank you for visiting my page. I'm a third-year Industrial Engineering & Economics student at UPC–UPF, currently gaining experience in Strategy & Consulting at Accenture as an intern.

I am passionate about problem-solving, business strategy, and understanding how organisations can create value and drive meaningful change, especially with AI's newest capabilities. Beyond consulting, I co-founded Guspira Lleure, a non-profit that runs summer camps for over 150 children each year — an experience that has strengthened my entrepreneurial mindset and my understanding of how organisations really work.

Outside university and work, I am an active scout leader and enjoy mountain sports, music, and learning new things.

My education path

I grew up in Castellcir, a small village where some of my closest friendships began. Looking back, those early school years taught me so much about people, relationships, and community.

Learn more about the journey+

I then moved to Institut Pedralbes in Barcelona for secondary school, where my interest in STEM subjects began to grow — especially mathematics. I have always been eager to learn, so I took part in as many projects and competitions as I could.

Later, I joined Institut Moisès Broggi, where I was selected among 25 students from more than 250 applicants to follow a demanding programme combining the International Baccalaureate and the Spanish Baccalaureate. That environment strengthened my analytical skills, broadened the way I approached problems, and showed me the value of excellence and hard work.

After those two great years, I chose a degree that combines two fields I find essential to understanding today's world: industrial engineering and economics. At UPC and UPF I am developing technical, analytical, and interpersonal skills through challenging coursework, constant teamwork, and applied projects.

Alongside my degree, I have explored new topics through an Ethics & AI course at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center, a Data Analysis & Visualization course at UPC School, and the McKinsey Forward programme. All of them have made me even more eager to keep learning.
